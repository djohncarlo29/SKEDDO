I want to clarify the landscape requirement because this is broader than fixing the black strip visible on the left in the screenshot.

There are effectively two physical landscape states:

1. Landscape with the system/status-bar/cutout side on the LEFT.
2. Landscape with the system/status-bar/cutout side on the RIGHT.

I do not want SKEDDO to have a special layout that only works correctly for whichever side Android happens to place the system area on.

The desired architecture is symmetrical:

- The SKEDDO background/surface should extend edge-to-edge across the entire physical display.
- The app's actual interactive content should remain inside the appropriate safe content bounds.
- Both the LEFT and RIGHT landscape edges should be treated as possible system-inset/cutout edges.
- If Android places the system area on the left, the content respects the left inset.
- If Android places it on the right, the content respects the right inset.
- The opposite side should also already be handled as an edge-to-edge/background boundary rather than producing a black/letterboxed region.
- The app should not need two completely different landscape layouts depending on which physical side the system area occupies.

In other words, I want the landscape window architecture to be symmetric and prepared for both landscape directions.

Please audit the Android window/inset configuration with that requirement specifically in mind.

Do not simply remove the current left inset.

Do not hard-code a left-side or right-side inset.

Do not add arbitrary padding to Flutter widgets.

Instead, determine how Android's edge-to-edge and display-cutout/window-inset APIs should be configured so that:

- the window/background occupies the entire display,
- the system/cutout areas are represented as safe insets,
- and Flutter receives the appropriate left/right safe-area information depending on which landscape direction the device is currently in.

Please verify both physical landscape directions on the actual Android device if possible.

The important distinction is:

Background edge-to-edge ≠ content edge-to-edge.

I want the former across both landscape edges, while the latter remains safely inset according to the actual system/window insets.

Also keep this completely separate from the picker geometry and rotation work. Do not modify "PickerGridGeometry" or the picker transition implementation to solve this.
