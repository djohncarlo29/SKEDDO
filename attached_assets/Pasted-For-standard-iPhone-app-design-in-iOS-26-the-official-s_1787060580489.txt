For standard iPhone app design in iOS 26, the official specifications from the [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars) outline the exact measurements for the bottom tab bar: [1, 2] 
## Standard Tab Bar Dimensions

* 
* Base Container Height: 49 to 50 points (the visual glass capsule itself). [3, 4] 
* Total Screen Footprint Height: 83 to 84 points. This includes the base container plus the 34-point bottom safe area space reserved for the home indicator slot. [4, 5] 
* Width: Variable. It typically matches the full device content width (e.g., 393 points on standard iPhones like the iPhone 16 or 15) minus responsive margins if floating. [4, 6, 7] 
* 

## Component & Text Specifications

* 
* Icon Size: 25 × 25 points for regular glyphs. For perfectly square icons, use 23 × 23 points.
* Label Text Size: 11 points (SF Pro, normal or medium weight). This acts as the standard minimum text size on native iPhone interfaces.
* Touch Target Height: Minimum 44 points vertically per tab to guarantee clean hit metrics. [3, 8, 9] 
* 

## Visual Spacing Rules

* 
* Icon-to-Label Gap: Typically 3 to 4 points of vertical separation.
* Max Items: A maximum of 5 tabs across the horizontal plane. Exceeding 5 drops item widths below 78 points, making touch selection difficult. [3, 4, 10] 
*
