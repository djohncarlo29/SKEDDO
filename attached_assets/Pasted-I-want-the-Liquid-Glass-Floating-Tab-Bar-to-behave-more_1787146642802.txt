I want the Liquid Glass Floating Tab Bar to behave more like an optical material with a spatially extended influence/capture area.

Specifically:

1. The glass should continue to react to the content directly behind it.


2. Content that is very close to the outside edges of the Floating Tab Bar should also be able to influence the appearance inside the glass.


3. For example, if a colorful object, text, icon, or bright area is immediately above/beside the tab bar but technically outside its bounds, its color/light/detail should be able to create a subtle refractive, diffused, or specular influence inside the glass.


4. Do not simply flip, mirror, or copy the nearby content into the glass. There should be no obvious upside-down text or recognizable mirrored objects. Right now, the mirroring something inside the bounds of the glass is upside down. Fix it.


5. The influence should be localized and fall off with distance: content immediately adjacent to the glass should have a stronger influence, while content farther away should have progressively less influence.


6. The effect should look like the glass is optically interacting with its surroundings, rather than displaying a transformed screenshot of the surroundings.



Technically, please investigate whether this can be achieved by capturing a larger backdrop region around the tab bar and using that extended capture as input to a displacement/refraction/distortion/specular compositing pass, while masking the final result to the actual glass shape.

The extended capture region should be used as a source for the optical effect, not directly displayed or geometrically mirrored.

Please preserve the existing tab bar's size, position, shape, animations, controls, and current Liquid Glass appearance. Only improve the optical interaction with nearby content.
