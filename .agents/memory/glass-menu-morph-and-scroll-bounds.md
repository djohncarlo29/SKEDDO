name: GlassMenu morph and scroll bounds
description: Keep menu transitions aligned and scroll indicators clear of rounded corners
---

## Rule
Toolbar menus, popovers, modal pickers, and long-press context panels use a foreground lens with peak thickness 30 and refractive index 1.45. Keep thickness full through 85% of each opening or closing morph, then ease it to zero over the final 15%; hold refraction at 1.45 during motion and return it to neutral 1.0 only at rest. Chromatic aberration retains its content-blur curve, peaking at 0.06. Keep the full content group at its settled screen position behind the live glass clip: counter-translate package-menu content against platter travel; for the independently placed long-press panel, preserve the inverse panel-scale compensation. Start 25% oversized with 4px blur at progress 0, then spring to scale 1 and blur 0; fade content in over the first 15% of opening and out over the final 15% of closing. Glass-style long-press panels use the toolbar menu's 350ms transition pace. Do not delay-mount, shrink, or move content with the surface. Filter the actual content once; do not blend the sharp source back into the refracted output, which creates duplicate glyphs. Every menu element, including the discard confirmation button, must remain inside the moving clip and foreground lens. Menu dismissal replays the opening spring, geometry, trigger fade, and blur ramp in reverse. Only Sort By and directional option rows with checkmark leading slots override their own horizontal inset to zero; parent rows and dividers retain a separate standard 12px inset, even when a menu sets its option-row inset to zero. Modal picker menus keep the standard 12px platter inset for both rows and dividers. For pinned toolbar menus, use the popover's 0.3 stretch amount, 0.08 resistance, and 4px rightward settled landing offset. The premium request remains capped by adaptive device quality. The menu scroll indicator should travel only along the straight-sided portion of its card, ending one corner radius from each outer edge.

**Why:** The user wants toolbar menus to morph like the circle-trigger discard confirmation and all menu contents to show foreground lens response without duplicate text. Mixing the sharp original with the refracted image creates visible ghost glyphs, so the shader must emit one refracted source; a restrained 0.06 channel offset keeps chromatic fringes without the double-image effect while the main displacement remains visible. The user requested the menu content to remain visibly oversized and blurred through the expanding clip, then fade only at the first/last 15% of its transition. Keep contents at their destination and inside the moving clip; the discard button must not escape into a root overlay. Reversing the same spring and blur path avoids the separate, visibly different close motion. A 16px checkmark edge gap applies only to Sort By option rows, not its parent or divider; separators need a separate inset so they are unaffected by option-row overrides.

**How to apply:** When changing `GlassMenu` morph behavior, compare its spring, path, blur ramp, render quality, layout-start timing, stretch, and content motion with `GlassPopover`; preserve the menu's existing radius and size. Keep the full target list mounted at transition progress 0, counter the moving platter's position with a local content offset, apply the shared group scale and blur, and rely on the current glass clip for reveal. Use the same open spring/curve in reverse for menu closes, and reverse the blur controller rather than freezing it. Keep popover actions—including the discard button—in the measured content subtree. Apply `contentHorizontalInset: 0` to individual Sort By option rows only; use the divider-specific inset for separators, never the row override. Derive the indicator track's top and bottom insets from the active menu radius.
