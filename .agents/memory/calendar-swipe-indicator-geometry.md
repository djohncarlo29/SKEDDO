---
name: Calendar swipe indicator geometry
description: Fixed-size selected and today indicators during Calendar swipes, including the separate blob deformation layer.
---

Selection bloom and swipe deformation are separate effects. User-driven calendar swipes must not start the incoming day's pre-bloom during the slide: the 55% pre-bloom makes the selected/today indicator visibly grow before release, especially at larger OS text scales. The selected and today backgrounds must remain in their existing day-circle render tree throughout a same-week content drag; translate only the selected background inside that tree. Multi-Day week-strip dates use two separate same-size circles; the timeline's current-time indicator remains a separate concern.

**Why:** The original product behavior uses a richer overshooting bloom for tap, re-tap, settle, and non-gesture navigation. Starting that animation during a user swipe creates the exact "grows while swiping, returns on release" symptom; larger scaled diameters make the same overshoot more obvious. A second root cause is the static-to-overlay handoff: replacing the mounted day-circle widget with a positioned Container changes the parent/compositing geometry at the first drag frame, which becomes visible once the scaled diameter is large enough to approach or exceed a day cell.

**How to apply:** Calendar day markers must always use their authored diameter, with the active OS TextScaler applied directly to that one baseline; do not derive circle geometry from each strip Text widget. Stop and reset the _BloomDayCircle controller before any fixed-size rebuild can trigger bloom, pulse, or settle; a transform clamp alone is not enough. Keep the day-number label at scale 1.0 and do not wrap the full-opacity swipe circle in the velocity-driven blob widget. During horizontal Day View motion, keep the same _BloomDayCircle mounted during a same-week content drag, translate the selected and adjacent circles together, keep today stationary (including the selected=today case), and paint the moving selected circle in a row-level foreground so it remains above today regardless of cell order. Use the number overlay only for text recoloring. Do not reintroduce a stretched week-strip pill; render the selected and adjacent Multi-Day dates as independent circles with the selected at full accent opacity and the adjacent circle at 40%.
