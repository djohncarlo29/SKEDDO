---
name: Landscape master-detail transition
description: Shared Settings and Events split behavior, pane geometry, safe-area reveal, and source-item anchoring.
---

The landscape Events master pane begins at the physical left edge, but its content receives the shared horizontal safe-area inset on the left. Its resting width is the portrait short-edge content width plus exactly one shared inset, leaving the visible master content equal to portrait width.

Entering a landscape Events DCV split must animate the master pane from the full landscape width to that resting width while the detail pane enters from the physical right. Preserve the original right safe-area clearance at the start, then shrink it with the master pane. Use one eased progress value for the master width, detail translation, header split, and divider so dismissal reverses the same motion.

In split mode, keep the hamburger and Add Category on the master header. Put the DCV back, ellipsis, and title in the sliding detail header; keep one shared Search/Plus pair fixed above that slide at the full header's trailing edge. The shared actions use the active DCV while it is open and the normal Events context otherwise. Do not duplicate those actions inside the DCV header.

While the split is active, animate the shared floating tab bar from screen center to the center of the portrait-width master content. The content starts at the left system inset, so its target is `horizontalInset + masterContentWidth / 2`; do not include the inset in the portrait-width calculation or center on the wider physical pane. Move its glass and separate shadow together. Returning to another tab or leaving the split targets normal screen center.

Keep right-pane content and DCV header controls laid out within the inset-safe content width, but extend the animated reveal clip through the trailing system inset. The inset is not a visual clipping boundary.

Settings must open in landscape as a full-width master panel. Open the detail pane only after a Settings row is tapped; place its back control at the right side of the detail header and use it to return to the full-width parent. When replacing a Settings subscreen, slide the complete detail-header page—surface, title, and controls—as one unit over the previous page. Settings and Events should use the same reusable master/detail transition behavior.

When a Settings row or Events category opens the detail pane from full width, preserve that source item as the master-pane focus anchor. Leave it at its current position if it remains visible above the tab bar; only center it when the narrower pane would otherwise hide it, and animate that correction alongside the pane transition. Do not recenter rows selected after the split is settled. Do not rely only on the old scroll offset because grid columns and row heights can change during the resize. A deliberate master-pane drag during the transition takes precedence over auto-centering; the next selected row becomes the new anchor. Preserve the master subtree when it moves between full-width and split layout so its scroll state does not remount.

**Why:** The tab bar's symmetric content-padding parent remains centered on the full window. Its target must account for the content's physical left origin while using only the portrait-width content to find that content's center. Centering on the inset-expanded pane places the tab bar left of the cards. Content must respect the system inset while its reveal viewport still reaches the physical edge, or the trailing icon is cut off during the transition. A header page's surface must travel with its title and controls; otherwise the text and chevrons overlap while the background stays behind. Per-frame scroll jumps make the master twitch; unconditional centering also moves rows that remain visible. Settings must not choose a default detail screen on open, and a scrolled source item must remain visible when the master narrows.

**How to apply:** Keep master-pane, content, and reveal-clip widths distinct. Compute the tab offset as `horizontalInset + (masterContentWidth - physicalWidth) / 2`, so the inset sets the content origin but is not counted as portrait content width. Keep Search/Plus outside the clipped detail-header transition, above it in the shell stack, and bind their callbacks to the active Events/DCV state. Apply the right inset to pane content, while the animated reveal clip includes the trailing inset. Use the shared transition for master width, detail reveal, and divider. During entry from full width, check whether the anchor would be obscured by the viewport or tab bar; only then animate it into view, and do not auto-center after the split has settled. For an intra-pane header change, paint the incoming page surface inside the same translated subtree as its text and controls so it covers the outgoing page as it moves in. Suppress correction after a deliberate user scroll until a new row is selected, and preserve the master subtree across layout branches.
