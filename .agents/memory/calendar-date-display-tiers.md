---
name: Calendar date display tiers
description: Product rules for Short and Medium date formats across calendar headers and modal date pills.
---

Use the selected Date Format's Medium style for modal date pills, the Single Day banner, dated Detailed Category View section headers, and the Day View shell title. Omit the year in the Multi Day banner and Day View shell title. The Full Month Name setting controls full month names versus three-letter abbreviations in Year View's mini-month labels and morphing labels as well as the Month View header.

**Why:** The user distinguishes the selected Short format from the more readable Medium display used on these surfaces. The Day View title must follow the selected date order while remaining yearless, and the shared header uses its space-separated shape to identify Day View and show its mode control.

**How to apply:** Keep modal pills, Single Day banners, and dated DCV headers on Medium with the year; omit the year for Multi Day banner labels and Day View shell titles. Keep the Year View month labels and their morph painter in sync with the Full Month Name setting. Leave other date surfaces unchanged unless the user expands the scope.
