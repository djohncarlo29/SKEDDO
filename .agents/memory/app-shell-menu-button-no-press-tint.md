description: Geometry, settings response, and press feedback for the AppShell's LiquidGlassButton.
---

The AppShell hamburger/X stays free of tap-time darkening or lightening, aligns
to the Notes attachment-preview close button, and follows the shared Liquid
Glass opacity setting. Keep its menu actions and tap behavior unchanged.

**Why:** The user wants consistent control placement and for this button's
glass strength to stay in sync with the Liquid Glass setting.

**How to apply:** Keep transparent splash/highlight settings scoped to this
control. Use the shared settings-based Liquid Glass appearance, and match the
preview close button's 40px circle at safeTop + 10px and horizontalInset + 16px.
