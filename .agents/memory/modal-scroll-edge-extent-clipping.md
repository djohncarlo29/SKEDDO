---
name: Modal scroll-edge extent and clipping
description: The top scroll-under effect's extent, sheet clipping, and transition compositing.
---

For modal scroll-under effects, derive both the top fade and blur extent from the scroll view's actual resting top content inset. They must both reach zero exactly at the first content card's top edge; do not use a fixed transition tail. Clip the effect with the containing sheet's actual top shape, including transparent corner cutouts. Popup sheets with a different radius must pass their own shape. Keep the scroller outside that clip so the backdrop filter can sample its content. During nested-sheet dismissal, checking that the edge widget exists or that its progress was restored does not prove the filtered pixels are visible.

Short content with a zero scroll range can overscroll in both directions. An upward touch drag may produce positive pixels beyond `maxScrollExtent`, moving the card beneath a fixed header; that positive overscroll must drive the top-edge blur just as a negative pull past `minScrollExtent` does. Short modal scrollers need always-scrollable bouncing physics so touch input can produce those metrics.

Nested rounded sheets must not install a delegated transition on the parent. Keep the parent sheet's own secondary transition responsible for its slide, scale, dim, and clip for the entire reverse-to-dismissed lifecycle. Flutter skips `receivedTransition` once the secondary animation is dismissed, so delegated and normal route transitions can otherwise change the backdrop filter's ancestor compositing at settle. When an edge disappears after nested dismissal, trace the actual route transition path and compare rendered pixels before presentation, during dismissal, after settling, and after a small parent scroll. Do not assume a temporary empty scroll measurement is the cause or rely on restoring a cached progress value alone; an instrumentation-only change is not a fix.

Keep `LiquidGlassScrollEdge` on its default `srcOver` composition. A transition-level `ColorFiltered` layer can make its backdrop blur disappear or render incorrectly. When one rounded modal sheet covers another, preserve the parent's dimming and clip the dim layer itself to the same top-rounded sheet shape. Keep the overlay inside the sheet's primary slide; do not put it beside an already-transitioned child, where its full-size bounds start at viewport y=0 instead of the sheet's visible top. Keep the top-level app-page dim alpha-aware (`srcATop`) so it does not tint transparent status-bar gaps.

The scroll-edge treatment and content rubberbanding should be available on Group and Edit Sections parent sheets, the Emoji Picker subsheet, and both Custom Repeat subsheets—not only the Category and Event parent sheets. Nested scrollers must feed their live position to the sheet effect.

On window-metric changes such as rotation, resync edge progress from the current laid-out scroll position after the frame, then advance a render revision on the LiquidGlassScrollEdge so its BackdropFilter layer is recreated even when progress is unchanged. Keep the scrollable and its controller mounted; retry the position read if dimensions are not ready.

**Why:** A short or independently tuned fade/blur stops before the first card, while an unclipped backdrop filter can paint into transparent rounded corners. The edge blur is sensitive to ancestor compositing, and route transitions have separate primary and secondary transforms; a sibling scrim outside the primary slide covers the status-bar gap above the sheet. Flutter 3.35.7 bypasses delegated transitions at `secondaryAnimation.isDismissed`, which changes the parent's transition wrapper exactly at settle. The project owner explicitly requires keeping the dim effect and fixing only its sharp-corner spill, then confirmed the explicit rounded scrim clip fixed it. A visually absent effect can survive state- and widget-level checks, and Flutter's software test renderer may not reproduce a device compositor failure.

**How to apply:** Use the measured first-content position as the single source for both fade and blur. Put only the full-size edge-effect overlay inside a `ClipPath` using the parent sheet's `ShapeBorder`; pass a custom shape for popups with a different radius. Place covered-sheet dimming inside the primary slide and explicitly clip the scrim itself to that same shape without changing its opacity. Keep the scroll-edge widget outside any transition `ColorFiltered` ancestor. For nested rounded sheets, opt out of delegated transitions and let the parent route's own secondary transition remain stable through dismissal. On zero-range content, derive short-pull progress from both `minScrollExtent - pixels` and `pixels - maxScrollExtent`; keep the shared effect enabled by default and use always-scrollable bouncing physics on short modal scrollers. Pass nested scroll controllers to the sheet effect, and ensure modal `CustomScrollView`s do not inherit the tab's primary controller. On metric changes, defer a live scroll-position sync until after layout and key only the backdrop-filter edge layer with a changing render revision; don't reset the scroll controller or depend solely on scroll notifications. Compare rendered pixels before presentation, during dismissal, after settling, and after a touch pull/release; use the actual live scroll position at settle rather than treating cached progress as the fix. Verify on the runtime/backend where the report occurs and follow up on a physical Android device when the issue is Android-specific.
