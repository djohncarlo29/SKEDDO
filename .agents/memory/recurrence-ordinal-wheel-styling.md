---
name: Recurrence ordinal wheel styling
description: Shared appearance rules for the Monthly and Yearly ordinal/day selectors in both repeat sheets.
---

Let the paired Monthly/Yearly ordinal and day wheels fill the available row so their connected selection bar spans the full width. Keep the option labels close together around the center seam by aligning the ordinal toward the right and the day toward the left. Retain barrel curvature and lowercase grouped labels `day`, `weekday`, and `weekend day`.

**Why:** The user wants a full-width selection bar like CupertinoDatePicker while keeping the paired labels close together and preserving the wheel effect.

**How to apply:** Keep the Category and Event repeat sheets consistent. Apply this to the Monthly “On the…” and Yearly day-selector wheels, not unrelated pickers or weekly weekday names.
