name: Recurrence ordinal wheel styling
description: Shared appearance rules for the Monthly and Yearly ordinal/day selectors in both repeat sheets.
---

Keep the paired Monthly/Yearly ordinal and day wheels as a compact, centered group sized from the widest displayed option. Center each option in its own wheel while retaining the barrel curvature. Display grouped selector labels as lowercase `day`, `weekday`, and `weekend day`.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected left/right edge alignment, excessive separation between the paired options, and sentence-case group labels while asking to preserve the wheel effect.

**How to apply:** Keep the Category and Event repeat sheets consistent. Apply this to the Monthly “On the…” and Yearly day-selector wheels, not unrelated pickers or weekly weekday names.
